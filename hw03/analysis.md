# HW3 Analysis: Executive Changes vs. Earnings Announcements

Based on my data, executive changes more often precede earnings
announcements than follow them: 22 of 30 events (73%) were filed before
the nearest earnings filing, 6 after, and 2 in the same week. Because
single filings often report several people (Walmart's January 16 filing
alone produced 5 rows), I also counted unique filings: 11 before,
4 after, and 1 same week, which is the same direction but a smaller
sample. The pattern varies by company: JPMorgan's and Walmart's changes
all came before or near earnings, while Microsoft was the exception, with
2 of 3 filings after earnings. The most notable timing was Walmart's CEO
succession announcement (Doug McMillon to John Furner), filed on
November 14, 2025, just 6 days before its Q3 earnings release. With only
16 filings across five companies over one year, this is suggestive rather
than conclusive, and "nearest earnings date" only measures timing, not
whether the two events are related.
