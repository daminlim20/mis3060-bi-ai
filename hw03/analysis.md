# HW3 Analysis: Executive Changes vs. Earnings Announcements

Based on my data, executive changes were more likely to happen before earnings announcements than after them. Out of 30 events, 22, or 73%, were filed before the nearest earnings filing, while 6 came after and 2 happened in the same week. Since some filings included several executives, I also looked at unique filings instead of individual people. By that measure, 11 filings came before earnings, 4 came after, and 1 occurred in the same week, so the overall pattern was still similar.

There were also some differences across companies. JPMorgan and Walmart had all of their executive changes either before or close to earnings, while Microsoft stood out because 2 of its 3 filings came after earnings. One example that stood out was Walmart’s CEO succession announcement from Doug McMillon to John Furner, which was filed on November 14, 2025, only six days before its Q3 earnings release.

Still, the sample is fairly small, with only 16 filings across five companies over one year, so I would treat this as a pattern rather than a firm conclusion. The “nearest earnings date” also only shows how close the events were in timing and does not necessarily mean that the executive change and earnings announcement were directly related.
